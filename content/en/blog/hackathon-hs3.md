From 13 to 15 November 2026 we are planning a special hackathon - 48 hours of joint work on hackerspace.

We focus on everything that can improve and develop HS3: infrastructure, automation, the look of the space, inventory, tidying, repairs, developing existing projects and new ideas.

For 48 hours we simply do Hackerspace. ✨🌌

This will be a hackathon without competition and without winners. We want the results of this work to remain in HS3 and genuinely improve its functioning.

Want to help? Join us!
- If you know HS3 and know what to do — drop by whenever you want!
- If you need an introduction, join us at one of the times marked in the schedule (⭐)
